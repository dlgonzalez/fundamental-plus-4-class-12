🗣 Speaking: Speed Networking Event

Let's actually PRACTICE networking! This is a real speed networking simulation.

🎯 Step 1: Create Your Networking Mission

Choose your mission for today's speed networking event:

Mission A: Same Career

Find someone who wants the same career as you

Mission B: Dream Company

Find someone who has a contact in your dream company

Mission C: Introduction

Find someone who can introduce you to someone else

Mission D: Experience

Find someone who has internship experience

Mission E: Advice

Find someone who can give you advice about job hunting

🎤 Step 2: Prepare Your Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

Your pitch must include:

  • Who are you?
  • What are you studying/looking for?
  • What do you need help with?
  • Use "probably/definitely" language
💬 Confident Communicator:
I will definitely... I'll probably... I definitely won't... I probably won't...

💡 Sample Pitch:

"Hi, I'm Carlos. I'm studying business administration and I'm definitely looking for an internship in marketing. I probably need help connecting with people in the industry."

⚡ Step 3: Speed Networking Rounds (15 minutes)

Instructions for the teacher:

  1. Set up 2 rows of chairs facing each other (or breakout rooms if online)
  2. Each round = 2 minutes
  3. Students introduce themselves and share their elevator pitch
  4. Try to complete their mission
  5. After 2 minutes, one row rotates to the next person
  6. Repeat 5-6 times

🎯 Debrief Questions:

  • Who completed their mission?
  • What was easy/hard about introducing yourself?
  • What will you DEFINITELY do after this class?
  • What will you PROBABLY do?
  • Who made a good contact? How will you stay in touch?

💡 Quick Teaching Tips
For the warm-up: The "Networking Do's & Don'ts" game gets students laughing while teaching real-world norms. The "CREEPY" option adds humor.

For vocabulary scenarios: Give students 5 minutes to prepare, then have pairs perform. Award bonus XP for the most creative dialogue.

For speed networking: Use a visible timer. Play soft background music to simulate a real event. After each round, have students write down ONE new contact they made.

For the network map: Encourage students to take a photo of their map and set it as a reminder to actually contact someone from their outer circle this week.
